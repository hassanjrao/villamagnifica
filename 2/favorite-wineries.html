Villa Magnifica's Favorite Wineries

  • Vitagliano:

  • 0.3 miles; 36101 Glen Oaks Rd D, Temecula, CA 92592
  • http://www.vitaglianowines.com/
  • Beautiful winery with a gorgeous lake. Adjacent to Villa Magnifica.

  • Bottaia:

  • .5 miles; 35601 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92591
  • https://www.bottaiawinery.com/
  • Brand new. Super modern. They have a great charcuterie board. They also have a great pool open to the public.

  • Monte the Oro:

  • 1.3 miles; 35820 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92591
  • https://www.pontewinery.com/
  • Very beautiful setting. Great views. Great wines. Light meals.

  • Wilson Creek Winery:

  • 1.5 miles; 35960 Rancho California Rd. Temecula, CA 92591
  • http://www.wilsoncreekwinery.com/
  • Great wines. Great place for lunch.

  • Ponte Winery:

  • 1.8 miles; 35053 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92592
  • https://www.pontewinery.com/
  • Great place for lunch and dinner. Great wines. Underground bar with food and live music.

  • Avensole Winery:

  • 2.6 miles; 4567 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92592
  • http://www.avensolewinery.com/
  • Super cool restaurant. You can see the cooks making the food. Very artistic.

  • Leoness Cellars:

  • 4.8 Miles; 38311 De Portola Rd, Temecula, CA 92592, USA
  • http://www.leonesscellars.com/
  • Great wines. Beautiful patio with great views and great food.

  • Robert Renzoni Winery:

  • 3.1 miles; 37350 De Portola Rd, Temecula, CA 92592
  • https://robertrenzonivineyards.com/
  • Great wine. Great food. Outdoor Restaurant.

  • Falkner Winery:

  • 3.5 miles; 40620 Calle Contento, Temecula, CA 92591
  • http://www.falknerwinery.com/index.php
  • Great wines, Great Service. Large restaurant with fantastic view.

  • Miramonte:

  • 3.8 miles; 33410 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92591
  • https://www.miramontewinery.com/
  • Have a full restaurant. Good food. Great view. Live music on some weekends.
