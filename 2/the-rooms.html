The Rooms

The Grand Room

  • Villa Magnifica boasts 20-foot ceilings with huge windows, making this spectacular home the brightest and happiest in all of Temecula!
  • The large windows throughout the home showcase the most magnificent private backyard in Temecula!
  • The Grand Room features the latest 75” 4K HDTV.

Dining Room

  • Our custom handcrafted dining table seats 18 guests, plus 4 additional guests at the center island.

The Kitchen

  • Villa Magnifica’s kitchen is fully equipped with a large refrigerator, double oven, two coffee machines, separate ice maker, two dishwashers, and a trash compactor. It includes top of the line cookware, with place settings for 24 guests.

Second Seating Area

  • Villa Magnfica features a second sitting area for guests to gather, watch TV or play popular board games.
  • For the younger generation, Villa Magnifica features a Play Station with many popular games.
  • Villa Magnifica even offers a custom Poker set for adult guests to play and try their luck well into the night!

The Bedrooms

  • 7 Bedrooms, 10 beds, 2 sofa beds
  • All bedrooms at Villa Magnifica feature the most comfortable mattresses and bedding, and walls adorned with beautiful vineyard artwork.
  • Each room at Villa Magnifica includes a wall-mounted, 4K Smart TV, complete with a free Netflix account and commercial-free Hulu.

The Bathrooms

  • The 5 full bathrooms at Villa Magnfica feature double sinks, beautiful mirrors, and plenty of light. The showers include 8 powerful jets and waterfall spouts, like those found at the most luxurious resorts!
  • The half bathroom (powder room) includes a large mirror and oversized countertop with lots of storage. Perfect for your guests to use during events!
