Floor Plan

Upstairs: Cabernet Room has a king bed; Merlot Room has a queen bed; Pinot Noir and Zinfandel Rooms each have two queen beds. The bedrooms connect through the upper hall, with stairs in the center and bathrooms beside the bedrooms.

Downstairs: the kitchen, island, dining area and living room occupy the upper part of the plan. Champagne Room has two queen beds; Chardonnay Room and the Bridal Suite each have a queen bed. The Game Room shows two queen sofa beds. Stairs and bathrooms are between the sleeping areas.
