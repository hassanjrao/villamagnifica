Team Bonding Experience

At Villa Magnifica

Villa Magnifica is the perfect place to host a series of hilarious Team Bonding games. These games will surely entertain your friends and family, whether it's for a corporate event or for the entire bridal party!

The cost for this 3 hour "Team Bonding" experience is $75 per person. This includes the coach, all props and items for the game, and medals for the winners.

The minimum number of people is 16, and the maximum is 40.

The Team Bonding experience requires one night stay at Villa Magnifica during the week, or two nights on weekends.

This experience can also be done at a public park without reserving Villa Magnifica.

Call 619-578-4013 for more information and to check availability.
