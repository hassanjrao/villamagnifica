Backyard

The Pool

  • Villa Magnifica features a beautiful lap pool with ever changing colored lights.
  • Two giant swan “floats” glide continually across the pool, adding to the great beauty of this wonderful paradise
  • The pool is shallow and perfect for pool games such a volleyball and waterpolo.

Bar & Grill

  • Villa Magnifica’s outdoor Bar & Grill features two top of the line grills, two burners, an ice chest, ice maker, refrigerator and a large sink

The Pizza Oven

  • Offer your guests a Pizza Bar with our top of the line outdoor Pizza Oven! Use this convenient gas-fired oven to bake delicious cookies or other baked goodies poolside.

The Pergola

  • Villa Magnifica features a covered Pergola with comfortable seating for 24 guests, along with 4 large heaters and 4 fire pits. Perfect for cooler evenings by the pool!

The Loggia

  • Villa Magnifica features a covered, lighted loggia with a dining room table and two comfortable sofas.

Poolside Deck

  • Villa Magnifica features a 400 sq. ft raised deck, with four cushioned lounge chairs for tanning by the pool. At night, solar lights provide a gorgeous and intimate setting on this deck.
  • This area can be also be used as a dance floor for your event

The Putting Green

  • Villa Magnifica features a putting green with sand trap and mound to challenge even the most seasoned golfer!

The Bocce Court

  • Villa Magnifica wouldn't be complete without a beautiful Bocce Court, the perfect entertainment area for the entire family and friends.

Backyard Games

  • Villa Magnifica includes 4,000 sqft of lighted turf to serve as your own private playground, complete with a variety of sports and games to satisfy all ages and levels of athletic prowess.
  • Here your guests can play backyard games such as Badminton, Paddle Ball, Volleyball, Soccer, Frisbee, WiffleBall, Corn Hole, Beer-Pong, Ping-Pong, and Giant Jenga.

The Parking

  • Villa Magnifica’s front yard has parking for up to 15 cars and includes a lovely lighted pathway that meanders through flowering trees and a fragrant rose garden.
