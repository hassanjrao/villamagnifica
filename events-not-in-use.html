Events

  • Villa Magnifica’s private backyard can be transformed from a sports court into a gorgeous venue for special events, with seating for up to 125 people. The several hundred outdoor lights around the property, and wonderful year-round breezes, turn Villa Magnifica into a magical wonderland at night. And there's no need to hassle with renting tables, chairs and linens, as Villa Magnifica will provide these for you at a much reduced rate!
  • Host private parties when you book the house for at least two nights. An event fee of $750 is charged for up to 50 guests, and $1,500 for parties over 50 guests, which includes a parking attendant. This fee covers the additional wear and tear of the house, parking attendant and additional cleaning required.
  • If planning a catered event, the catering company must be an approved vendor of Villa Magnifica. Additional banquet tables, linens and chairs for your events are available at a reduced price compared to other rental companies in the area. Please contact the host to discuss your event.
  • Prior to booking this beautiful home, please refer to the House Rules and contact the host with any questions. Do not book this home unless you completely agree with all the rules.
